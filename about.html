ORIGINS & ESSENCE

THE 1928 STUDIO STORY

A nearly century-old legacy of architectural design rigor, reimagined for modern cultural leadership.

"Great design is never decoration. It is the gravitational center of market authority."

Founded on the principle that enduring brands require architectural discipline rather than fleeting trends, 1928 operates at the convergence of identity systems, spatial design, and high-velocity digital technology.

From our headquarters in Ahmedabad to collaborations across London, Milan, Tokyo, and New York, our studio engineers timeless visual authority for ambitious founders and global icons.
