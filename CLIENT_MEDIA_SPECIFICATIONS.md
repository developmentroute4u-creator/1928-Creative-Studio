## 6. ASSET COMPRESSION & OPTIMIZATION TIPS

* **Images:** Free tools like [TinyPNG / TinyJPG](https://tinypng.com) or [Squoosh.app](https://squoosh.app) compress PNG and JPEG files without any visible loss of quality.
* **Videos:** Free tools like [Handbrake](https://handbrake.fr) (preset: *Web Optimized 1080p30*) reduce large video files from 200MB down to 15MB while keeping full HD clarity.
* **Vector Graphics:** Export logos directly from Illustrator / Figma as `.svg` with paths converted to outlines.
